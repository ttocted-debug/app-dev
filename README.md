## Series Title: *Dragon Ball Z*


### Summary
*Dragon Ball Z* is a Japanese anime series that follows **Goku** and his friends as they defend the Earth from powerful enemies like **Frieza**, and **Majin Buu**.    

### ⚔️ Main Characters  
- **Goku** – The Saiyan warrior and main hero.  
- **Vegeta** – The proud Prince of all Saiyans.  
- **Gohan** – Goku’s eldest son with hidden potential.  
- **Piccolo** – A former enemy turned ally.  
- **Frieza** – One of the most iconic villains in anime history.
